A Recipe
to Stay Awake
in Class

Secret ingredient and procedures that allows you to stay awake and present for every class.
- ZOE C:

Step 1

DRINK
COFFEE.

Less than eight hours of sleep?

Additional caffeine is needed
(+1 can of energy drink / +1 shot of expresso / +1 cup of coffee).

Step 2

PUT ON FULL
MAKEUP.

Having invested this much effort in appearing awake,
tons of makeup make you uncomfortable enough to fall asleep.

Step 3

WEAR
the TIGHTEST CLOTHES.

The discomfort keeps you aware of your physical existence
and prevents your consciousness from drifting too far away.

Step 4

PLAY
MUSIC.

Keep something playing loudly on your way to class.
Travis Scott, Kanye West, The Weeknd, “Million Dollar Baby”—anything that temporarily gives you an unreasonable amount of confidence.

Step 5

RAISE YOUR HAND TO SPEAK UP

Answer one question, or make a comment in class aloud
to allow the embarressment to rush through your spine.
